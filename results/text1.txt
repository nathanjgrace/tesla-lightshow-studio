In the Closures rows of the timeline, click a beat cell to cycle its command: Open and Close actuate the panel, Dance wiggles it open & shut, and Stop halts it mid-motion. Click again past Stop to clear. Dimmed cells are auto-choreographed for you; click any cell to override it.
Auto-choreograph closures to the music: on by default, opening your car's falcon/front doors to land open on the drops and dance through big sections, automatically within Tesla's limits. Uncheck to keep your show lights-only.
⚠ Real doors/closures will move, so make sure your Tesla has clearance. Your Tesla will also ask you to confirm on its screen before the show runs.
How to use on your Tesla
Click Export ZIP to download the LightShow package. We email you the same link either way.
Copy the LightShow/ folder to the root of a USB drive (exFAT or FAT32, not NTFS). This step needs a computer.
Plug the USB into your Tesla's front USB or glovebox port.
Tap Toybox → Light Show → Schedule Show on the touchscreen. Start it there, not from the phone app: owners of 2025+ Model Ys report the app can skip the front lights.
