# 3D car models

The models in this folder were made by the people below and adapted for Lightshow Studio (scaled, split into moving parts, lamps mapped to light show channels, compressed) with `scripts/models`. They are **not** covered by the repository's AGPL license: each keeps its own Creative Commons license. The app shows the credit next to the car.
