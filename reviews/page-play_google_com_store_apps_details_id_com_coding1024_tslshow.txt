With our app, you can effortlessly create custom light shows synced to your favorite tunes, turning heads wherever you go. No technical skills needed—just select your music and watch the magic happen. Features: Auto-sync lights to music beats Adjustable flashing frequency and duration Easy manual frame editing Preview and export for xLights Special Offer: Free Tesla accessory trials included! How to Use: Share an mp3 or wav music file. Tap auto to see your light show in action. Export and check file size limits. Share files and copy to a USB drive's "LightShow" folder. Insert USB into your Tesla and dazzle the crowd! USB Requirements: "LightShow" folder with "lightshow.fseq" and "lightshow.mp3/wav" Format: exFAT, FAT 32, MS-DOS (Mac), ext3/ext4. NTFS not supported. No TeslaCam or firmware update files. Supported Models: Model Y Model 3 Model 3 Highland Model S (2021+) Model X (2021+) Disclaimer: Absolutely protect your privacy, no personal info collected. Creates light show files only; does not control your vehicle. Test on select Tesla models; caution with other brands. Tesla® is a registered trademark of Tesla, Inc.
