# Research Notes: Factory Paint Colors

_Compiled 2026-10-19. Used only by the 3D preview's paint picker (`src/core/paint.ts`); nothing here affects exported shows._

Confidence tags as in `01-tesla-lightshow-format.md`: **[verified]** = from a Tesla document, **[doc]** = reported by a reliable outlet, **[unverified]** = from memory or inferred; check against Tesla's configurator or a service manual before relying on it.

Hex values are visual approximations picked to read correctly under the preview's night lighting. They are **not** paint codes.
